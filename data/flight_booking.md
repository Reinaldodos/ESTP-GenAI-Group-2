# Flight Booking Rules

Employees should use the approved booking channel after authorization. The itinerary should balance cost, journey time, and business needs. A cheaper option is not required if it creates an overnight connection or unreasonable safety concern.

Flights of six hours or less must normally be booked in economy class. A flight longer than six hours may be booked in business class. The duration test applies to an individual scheduled flight segment, not to the combined duration of several shorter connecting flights.

Premium economy may be selected for a flight longer than four hours when its fare is no more than EUR 150 above the lowest reasonable economy fare. Manager approval is required before booking premium economy. This option does not change the separate rule that permits business class on a segment longer than six hours.

Travelers should compare practical nonstop and connecting options. An alternative is reasonable when it meets the business schedule and adds no more than two hours. A nonstop option may cost up to EUR 100 more than a connecting option.

Standard seat selection is reimbursable. Extra-legroom seats require manager approval unless they are needed for an approved medical or accessibility accommodation. Charges for preferred boarding, lounge access, onboard entertainment, or in-flight internet are personal unless a documented business requirement makes them necessary.

The company reimburses one standard checked bag when it is needed for a business trip. Additional baggage for personal belongings is not reimbursable. Fees for business equipment or event materials may be claimed when the manager approved the need before travel.

Employees may add a personal destination or personal dates to an itinerary. Before booking, they must obtain a comparison showing the cost of the business-only itinerary at the same time. The company pays no more than that comparison amount, and the employee pays any increase caused by the personal portion.

Unused tickets and airline credits purchased by the company remain company property. Changes and cancellations should be processed through the original booking channel so that any available refund or credit can be recorded.
