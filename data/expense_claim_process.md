# Expense Claim Process

Employees must submit travel expense claims within 30 days after returning. One claim should normally cover one trip and identify its purpose, destination, dates, and approving manager. Corporate-card and personally paid items must both be included.

Each expense should show the transaction date, amount, currency, category, and business reason. An itemized receipt is required for accommodation, transportation, and any individual expense of EUR 25 or more. Finance may request a receipt for a lower amount when the nature of the purchase is unclear.

For a missing receipt, the employee must provide a written declaration describing the supplier, date, amount, items purchased, business purpose, and reason the receipt is unavailable. A card statement may support the declaration but does not replace itemization. Manager approval of the declaration is required.

Meal reimbursement is limited to EUR 50 per day for breakfast, lunch, and dinner combined. The limit applies to the employee's own meals and includes taxes and reimbursable tips. Restaurant tips are reimbursable up to 10% of the meal cost when tipping is customary in the country visited.

When a host, conference, hotel, or carrier supplies a meal, other reasonable meals may be claimed that day, but the EUR 50 total still applies. Meals for external guests are business hospitality and require the guest names and business purpose.

Foreign-currency expenses must be converted to euros using the exchange rate shown on the employee's credit card statement or the official exchange rate on the purchase date. The employee should record the original currency and amount and identify the conversion method used.

The employee must separate personal costs from the claim, including companion expenses, leisure activities, personal itinerary changes, and non-business hotel nights. If a receipt combines business and personal items, only the business portion should be entered and the allocation should be explained.

The manager reviews business purpose, policy compliance, and budget ownership. Finance reviews documentation, tax treatment, currency conversion, and duplicate claims. A returned claim should be corrected promptly; returning a claim for information does not extend the original 30-day submission deadline.
