# Group Travel, Events, and Conferences

Conference, trade-event, and group-meeting travel requires manager approval. The request should identify the event, purpose, registration and travel costs, and the benefit of each employee attending.

Approved registration fees are reimbursable. Social events, excursions, guest passes, and charitable additions are personal unless a manager documents a business purpose before purchase. Early rates should be used when plans are certain.

For five or more employees at one event, an organizer should coordinate dates and bookings with the travel department. Each traveler remains responsible for registration, expenses, and individual approval.

Organizers should compare individual and group bookings. A lower group rate should not be selected if restrictive cancellation terms create a greater likely total cost.

Employees should share ground transportation when practical. A larger group vehicle may be booked at reasonable total cost. Premium vehicles chosen for comfort or status are not reimbursable.

Accommodation booked through an event housing service remains subject to the normal EUR 180 nightly limit. A conference hotel above the limit requires manager approval before booking, even when the organizer recommends it. The proximity of the venue and the cost of alternative transport may support an exception.

Meals included in a conference fee or group booking should not be claimed again as separate expenses. Other reasonable personal meals remain subject to the combined EUR 50 daily allowance. A group dinner with external guests must be recorded as business hospitality with the attendees and business purpose identified.

If one employee pays a permitted group cost, the expense claim must identify the participants and explain why centralized payment was used. Other participants must not claim the same cost. Personal extensions before or after the event must be separated using a business-only cost comparison.

Approved event materials and necessary shipping may be reimbursed. Employees must not use personal baggage allowances to conceal commercial goods or avoid customs requirements.
