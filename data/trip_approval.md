# Trip Approval and Changes

All business trips require approval from the employee's manager before any reservation is made. The request must explain the business purpose, destination, travel dates, expected transport, and estimated total cost. An invitation or meeting agenda should be attached when one is available.

Employees should submit domestic trip requests at least three working days before departure. International trips require more preparation and must also be registered with the travel department at least five working days before departure. Late requests may be considered when the business need could not reasonably have been anticipated.

The manager confirms that the trip is necessary, that the cost is proportionate to the expected business benefit, and that the expense can be charged to an available budget. Approval of a trip does not remove the employee's responsibility to follow the individual limits in the travel and expense policies.

A department head must approve a trip when its estimated total cost exceeds EUR 3,000. The finance team must review a request when the employee proposes a non-standard payment arrangement or an advance. These reviews are additional to manager approval, not substitutes for it.

Travelers may make minor changes after approval when the total estimate does not increase by more than 10% and the business purpose and destination remain the same. A date change that adds a personal stay is allowed, but the company pays only the cost that would have applied to the business itinerary.

A new approval is required when the destination changes, the total estimate rises by more than 10%, business-class travel is added, or the trip is extended for an additional business event. The employee should update the request before accepting the revised booking whenever circumstances permit.

If a trip is cancelled, the traveler must notify the manager and the travel department promptly. Refundable bookings should be cancelled through the original booking channel. Credits issued in the company's name must be retained for future business travel and must not be used for a personal trip.

Emergency travel may begin before written approval only when delay would create a material safety, legal, or operational risk. The traveler must obtain verbal manager agreement where possible and document the reason within two working days.
