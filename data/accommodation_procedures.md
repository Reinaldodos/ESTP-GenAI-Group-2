# Accommodation Procedures

Employees should use the approved booking channel when a suitable option is available. The property should be safe, appropriate, and reasonably close to the business location. Its cost should be considered alongside local transportation.

The normal accommodation limit is EUR 180 per night. This limit applies to the room rate and mandatory property charges but excludes separately stated taxes. Breakfast included in the room price does not count against the daily meal allowance. Optional room upgrades and paid leisure facilities are personal expenses.

A room above EUR 180 per night requires manager approval before booking. An exception may be reasonable during a major local event, when security or accessibility needs restrict the available choices, or when a more distant hotel would make the combined room and transportation cost higher. The traveler should retain evidence of the available rates.

Employees should use a negotiated hotel when its location, current rate, and conditions are suitable. Another compliant hotel may be used when it provides better overall value. Travelers must confirm the current rate before booking.

The reimbursable stay normally begins on the night before the first business activity and ends on the day of the final activity. An additional business night may be approved when available transportation would otherwise require unsafe travel hours or would cost more than the extra accommodation.

Personal extensions are permitted, but the employee must pay for personal nights and any increase to the business portion of the booking. A price comparison should be retained when combining personal and business dates. The company does not reimburse charges for companions.

Standard internet access is reimbursable when it is not included in the room rate and is needed for work. Minibar items, films, spa access, laundry for trips shorter than seven consecutive nights, and other personal services are not reimbursable. Necessary laundry may be claimed on a trip of seven consecutive nights or longer with a receipt.

Travelers should cancel unused rooms before the deadline. A missed-night charge may be reimbursed after a documented business change, disruption, illness, or emergency. Avoidable late-cancellation charges are personal unless a manager approves an exception.
