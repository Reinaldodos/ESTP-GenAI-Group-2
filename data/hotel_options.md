Recommended Hotel Options

The company has negotiated rates with several hotels in major business destinations. Employees should use these hotels when the location and price are suitable.

Amsterdam

Canal View Hotel
€165 per night. Located near Amsterdam Central Station. Suitable for business trips and includes breakfast and Wi-Fi.

Business Park Hotel
€145 per night. Located near the Amsterdam Zuid business district. Includes Wi-Fi and access to a small fitness center.

London

Westminster Business Hotel
€175 per night. Located near Westminster. Includes Wi-Fi and breakfast.

London City Hotel
€160 per night. Located near the City of London. Includes Wi-Fi but breakfast is not included.

Paris

Seine Business Hotel
€170 per night. Located near the business district of La Défense. Includes Wi-Fi and breakfast.

Central Paris Hotel
€185 per night. Located near central Paris. Breakfast is included.

Because the standard hotel limit is €180 per night, employees need manager approval before booking the Central Paris Hotel.

Berlin

Berlin Mitte Hotel
€150 per night. Located in central Berlin. Includes Wi-Fi and breakfast.

Airport Business Hotel
€125 per night. Located near Berlin Brandenburg Airport. Includes Wi-Fi but breakfast is not included.

Hotel prices are listed as examples for this exercise and may change. Employees should check the current negotiated rate before booking.
