# Corporate Cards and Travel Advances

Employees who have a corporate card should use it for approved business travel expenses wherever the card is accepted. The card is intended for transportation, accommodation, meals, and other necessary business costs. Using the card does not by itself make an expense reimbursable; every purchase must still comply with policy.

Corporate cards must not be used for personal purchases. An accidental personal charge must be reported to finance, repaid, and identified as personal in the expense claim. Repeated or deliberate personal use may result in card suspension.

The cardholder is responsible for protecting the card and its credentials. A lost, stolen, or compromised card must be reported immediately to the card issuer and to finance. Suspicious transactions should not be approved merely to complete an expense report; they should be disputed through the issuer's process.

Itemized receipts are required under the normal expense rules even when a transaction appears on the corporate card statement. The statement proves payment but may not show what was purchased. The employee must explain any missing receipt and provide alternative evidence when possible.

Cash withdrawals on a corporate card are prohibited unless finance has approved an exception for a destination where electronic payment is not practical. Any approved cash must be accounted for with receipts and unused funds must be returned.

An employee without a corporate card may use a personal payment method and request reimbursement. Expenses paid in a foreign currency must be converted to euros using the exchange rate on the employee's card statement or the official exchange rate on the purchase date. Foreign transaction fees directly associated with an approved business purchase are reimbursable.

A travel advance may be requested when substantial necessary costs must be paid in cash or when personal payment would create unreasonable hardship. The request requires manager and finance approval and should be submitted at least five working days before the funds are needed.

The advance must be reconciled in the expense claim within 30 days after the employee returns. Any unused balance must be repaid at the same time. An employee with an overdue advance may not receive another advance until the earlier amount has been settled.
