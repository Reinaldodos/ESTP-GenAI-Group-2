Business Travel Policy

Employees may travel for business when the trip is approved by their manager.

Flights longer than six hours may be booked in business class. Flights of six hours or less should be booked in economy class.

Employees should normally book hotels with a nightly cost of no more than €180. Exceptions require manager approval.

Employees may extend a business trip for personal travel, but the company will only pay the costs that would have been incurred for the business portion of the trip. Additional personal hotel nights and transportation costs are the employee's responsibility.

All international business trips must be registered with the travel department at least five working days before departure.

Employees should use public transportation when it is practical. Taxis may be used when public transportation is unavailable, unsafe, or would cause a significant delay.

Employees are responsible for ensuring that their passport, visa, and other required travel documents are valid before an international trip.
